Our Journey

Paris Airport Transfers — The Original #1

We were the very first to offer dedicated airport transfer services to and from Paris — long before it became a trend. From day one, our mission has been clear: deliver a premium, in-house experience with zero subcontracting, complete reliability, and unmatched customer care.

"STP was built on the idea that airport transfers deserve better. No third parties. No compromises. Just our team, our vehicles, and our standards — fully under our control."

Today, we proudly operate with a 100% in-house team, offering fixed pricing, trained staff, and a seamless experience trusted by thousands of travelers. This approach has made us the benchmark in airport transportation across Paris.

Our Foundation

Mission & Core Values

Guided by principles that put customers first

Safety First

We prioritize the safety of our passengers and drivers above all else. All our vehicles undergo rigorous maintenance checks, and our drivers are thoroughly vetted and trained.

Reliability

We understand that punctuality matters. Our commitment to timely pickups and efficient routes ensures you reach your destination on schedule, every time.

Customer Focus

Your satisfaction drives everything we do. We continuously seek feedback and make improvements to enhance your experience with BSD Transport.

Sustainability

We're committed to reducing our environmental impact by gradually transitioning to a fleet of hybrid and electric vehicles and implementing eco-friendly practices.

Innovation

We embrace technology to make your ride experience smoother and more enjoyable, from our user-friendly app to our advanced ride tracking system.

Community

We're more than just a taxi service; we're part of the community. We actively participate in local initiatives and give back through various community programs.
